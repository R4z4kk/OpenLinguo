# jieba — proper names absent from the lexicons

Generated by `tools/data-pipeline` from fxsjy/jieba@67fa2e3. Do not edit by hand.

- Source: `jieba/dict.txt` of https://github.com/fxsjy/jieba at commit 67fa2e36e72f69d9134b8a1037b83fbb070b9775 (the last commit; the project is frozen since 2020). Built by its author from the People's Daily 1998 segmented corpus, the MSR segmentation corpus and novels segmented with ICTCLAS (jieba issue 7), with words from the Sogou 2006 lexicon (Changelog).
- Author: Sun Junyi
- License: MIT (`jieba-MIT.txt`, copied from the `LICENSE` file at that commit)
- Changes: this data has been modified. Only the words tagged as proper names are kept, without their frequency, as JSON shards of `[word, kind]` sorted by word: `person` (tags `nr`, `nrfg`), `place` (`ns`), `organization` (`nt`), `name` (`nrt`, transliterated names of people and places alike, and `nz`, other proper names). Words whose form is a simplified or traditional headword of CC-CEDICT, CFDICT, the French Wiktionary or the English Wiktionary extended lexicon are skipped and counted. Words not only in Chinese characters are left out and listed in `issues.tsv`.
- Use: an offline signal that an unknown sequence is probably a proper name, shown as a proposal, never as a fact. jieba gives no meaning and no reading. Measured on the UD Chinese-GSDSimp treebank (train, dev and test), among the multi-character tokens absent from every shipped lexicon that jieba tags as a name, 87% are proper nouns (336 of 385): `nrt` 97%, `nr` 89%, `ns` 82%, `nz` 75%; the word frequency makes no difference, so none is kept.
- Result: 89004 names kept (63398 nr, 410 nrfg, 9787 ns, 3898 nt, 4348 nrt, 7163 nz); skipped: 23116 already in a lexicon; issues: 7 non-han-title.
