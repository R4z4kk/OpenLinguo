- Underlying sources of the Chinese list: Wikipedia; OPUS OpenSubtitles 2018 (data from OpenSubtitles, http://www.opensubtitles.org/) and SUBTLEX-CH (Cai, Q., & Brysbaert, M. (2010), SUBTLEX-CH: Chinese word and character frequencies based on film subtitles, PLoS One 5(6), e10729; SUBTLEX is freely available data); NewsCrawl 2014 and GlobalVoices; Google Books Ngrams 2012 (http://books.google.com/ngrams); OSCAR web text; Twitter; the word list shipped with the Jieba segmenter
- Changes: this data has been modified. Frequency buckets are converted to JSON shards of `[word, Zipf frequency]` (Zipf = log10 of the occurrences per billion words = 9 - bucket / 100), most frequent first; only the simplified headwords of CC-CEDICT, CFDICT and French Wiktionary are kept. wordfreq stores Chinese in its "oversimplified" form (traditional characters replaced by simplified ones), and its Chinese segmentation comes from Jieba, so a word Jieba splits has no frequency here.
- Result: 88668 of 130510 dictionary headwords have a frequency (334609 words in the list).
