# Design system — Ink and tone

Approved by the maintainer on 2026-10-09 (issue #9), from a private design canvas with the token sheet and eight key mobile screens. Implementation starts with the PWA shell (#22). The M4 writing and typing screens (issue #152) are on two more pages of the same canvas, described under [Writing and typing](#writing-and-typing-m4).

## Principle

The interface is monochrome, ink on paper. Color carries one meaning only: the Mandarin tones. Buttons and links are never colored, so a green always means "tone 3". Tone is always written by its diacritic (ā á ǎ à); color doubles it and never carries it alone (WCAG 1.4.1).

## Interface colors

Contrast ratios are WCAG 2.x (relative luminance per WCAG 2.2), against the ground named in each cell. The values live in `apps/web/src/styles.css` as `light-dark(light, dark)`; `apps/web/src/design/tokens.test.ts` checks every ratio below in CI, and that `ink`, `muted` and `danger` stay ≥ 4.5:1 and `border-strong` ≥ 3:1 on `bg`, `surface` and `sunken` in both themes.

| Token | Light | Dark | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| `bg` | `#F6F7F5` | `#121514` | Page ground |
| `surface` | `#FFFFFF` | `#1A1E1C` | Cards, sheets, navigation |
| `sunken` | `#EEF0ED` | `#232826` | Tracks, selected rows, inset blocks |
| `hairline` | `#DADDD8` | `#2E3431` | Decorative separators only |
| `border-strong` | `#7E8580` (3.5:1 on `bg`) | `#7A827E` (4.3:1 on `surface`) | Control boundaries (WCAG 1.4.11) |
| `ink` | `#191C1A` (16.0:1 on `bg`) | `#ECEFED` (15.9:1 on `bg`) | Text, primary buttons, focus ring |

## Tone colors

| Tone | Light | Contrast on white | Dark | Contrast on `bg` |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 · high | `#AF2D0B` | 6.6:1 | `#DF6445` | 5.3:1 |
| 2 · rising | `#A20B9F` | 6.8:1 | `#AB6AE3` | 5.2:1 |
| 3 · dipping | `#0B796E` | 5.3:1 | `#71E1BB` | 11.5:1 |
| 4 · falling | `#0619DB` | 9.7:1 | `#A3B2DD` | 8.7:1 |
| neutral | `#5F6662` | 5.9:1 | `#A3ABA6` | 7.8:1 |

**Tone-colored text sits on `bg` or `surface` only**, never on `sunken`: in the dark theme tones 1 and 2 reach only 4.3:1 and 4.2:1 on `sunken` (decision 2026-10-09, the colors are kept). A selected row or inset block that shows colored pinyin uses an outline instead of `sunken`. Every tone is ≥ 4.7:1 on `bg` and `surface` in both themes (checked in CI). Tone 2 stays purple in both themes so a learner keeps one color per tone.

**Colorblind separation.** The palette was found by a search maximizing the smallest color difference (CIE76 ΔE in Lab) between any two tones, under normal vision and simulated protanopia, deuteranopia and tritanopia (Machado, Oliveira and Fernandes 2009, severity 1.0 matrices applied to linear sRGB and clamped, then CIE Lab with a D65 white), within the contrast constraints and the usual hue families (red-orange, purple, green-teal, blue).

| Smallest ΔE between two tones | Normal | Protanopia | Deuteranopia | Tritanopia |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Light | 52.7 | 37.5 | 31.5 | 30.6 |
| Dark | 54.8 | 38.2 | 30.4 | 32.6 |

For comparison, an Okabe-Ito-derived starting palette dropped to 8.6 under protanopia. The ΔE values above are recomputed by the CI test (they differed by up to 0.4 from the search's own figures, corrected 2026-10-09), which also requires every simulated deficiency to stay ≥ 30.

## Typography

| Role | Typeface | License |
|---|---|---|
| Latin interface | Atkinson Hyperlegible Next (Braille Institute, designed for legibility) | OFL-1.1 |
| Chinese interface and pinyin (all tone diacritics) | Noto Sans SC | OFL-1.1 |
| Display characters (word page, cards, strokes) | LXGW WenKai GB, a Kai face close to handwriting with the mainland standard character forms (通用规范汉字表) taught for the HSK (v1.522, decision 2026-10-09) | OFL-1.1 |
| Fallback display | Noto Sans SC (already loaded; Noto Serif SC dropped, 2026-10-09) | OFL-1.1 |
| Rare characters of the CJK extension planes that the fonts above lack (end of every stack) | OpenLinguo Han Fallback, modified from Plangothic (Source Han Sans with mainland forms), Regular only (decision 2026-10-12, #67) | OFL-1.1 |
| Phonetic transcriptions (IPA) | The device's system font: Atkinson Hyperlegible Next and the Noto Sans SC slices have no IPA glyphs (checked 2026-10-11), so a transcription is drawn by one font rather than mixed | — |

All fonts are self-hosted; no third-party font CDN at runtime (GDPR). The CJK fonts are cut by `tools/data-pipeline` into `unicode-range` slices that follow the HSK 2025 bands (Latin and pinyin, punctuation, symbols, HSK 1 … 7-9, GF0025-2021, then the other dataset characters by frequency), so an HSK 1 page loads 67 KB of Noto Sans SC and 51 KB of LXGW WenKai GB, against 368 KB and 587 KB with frequency-ordered slices (measured 2026-10-09, budget checked in CI by `apps/web/src/design/fonts.test.ts`).

## Space, shape, motion

- Spacing on a 4 px step: 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64.
- Radius: 6 px for controls, 12 px for cards, full pill for chips.
- Touch targets ≥ 44 px (above the WCAG 2.5.8 minimum of 24 px). Words inside a text in the reader follow its line height, as 2.5.8 allows for targets in a sentence (ADR 0014).
- Motion: 150 ms ease-out; removed under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Focus: 2 px ink ring, 2 px offset, never hidden by sticky elements.

## Information architecture

- Mobile: bottom tabs Today / Learn / Read / Profile. From 1024 px: sidebar.
- **Today** runs the composed session from one button: reviews → new words → tone drill → 5-sentence challenge.
- Gamification stays quiet: streak and XP chips in the Today header, league line under the session. No loss messages, streak freeze, notifications opt-in only. Leagues use pseudonyms and predefined avatars.

## Screen patterns

- **Review**: progress bar in words, « X sur N mots », N fixed when a card comes back (ADR 0013), one card, grading buttons À revoir / Difficile / Bien / Facile with next intervals; "Bien" is the single filled button.
- **Card types**: recognition, recall (French → Chinese), listening, tone choice (four buttons with diacritic, color and tone number), writing (draw the strokes on the 米 grid, or write on paper, reveal the stroke order and self-grade), typing (in-app pinyin IME with a candidate list).
- **Word page**: pinyin, audio, FR and EN glosses, decomposition as a nested list (word → characters → components), stroke count and stroke order.
- **Pronunciation studio**: reference (dashed) and user (solid) pitch curves, per-syllable text result, sandhi hint, on-device processing notice, "Je ne peux pas parler maintenant".
- **Writing studio**: five contexts, AI correction shown as a labeled proposal ("Généré par IA") with inserted characters underlined, accept or keep, optional card creation.
- **Reader**: unknown words dotted-underlined, tapped word outlined, word sheet at the bottom; see [Reader (M6)](#reader-m6).
- **Profile**: streak, retention, words learned, time; league table with the user's row highlighted; settings for AI provider, display, data and privacy (export, delete).
- **Stats heatmap**: 12 weeks of learning days in three shades, `sunken` (no review), `border-strong` (reviews) and `ink` (daily goal met), each ≥ 3:1 from its neighbour in both themes (checked in CI). The same numbers follow as a table in a disclosure (WCAG 1.4.11 lets a graph rely on "a table that follows the graph").

## Writing and typing (M4)

Mocked up for issue #152 from [ADR 0011](../adr/0011-writing-and-typing.md): every state on a phone (390 px) and a desktop (1280 px), light and dark, each with its focus order. The copy is French, from `apps/web/src/i18n/fr.ts` where a string exists. Stroke shapes come from `data/hanzi-writer-data`; the input method's candidates are ranked from CC-CEDICT and wordfreq as the ADR specifies.

- **Writing card, draw mode**: meaning, pinyin and audio above the word page's 米 grid, without outline, framed in `border-strong` because it takes input (WCAG 1.4.11); 280 px on a phone, 320 px from 1024 px. Boxes after "Caractère 1 sur 2" show the progress through the word, a finished character in its box. A right stroke settles into its true shape in ink; the status line (`aria-live="polite"`) names the stroke, never how many are left. A miss says "pas tout à fait", a reversed stroke "tracé à l'envers". After three misses the stroke's outline appears, dashed `border-strong` over `sunken`, with an `ink` dot at its start, without motion under reduced motion. Three misses more and the stroke is drawn for the learner ("tracé pour vous"), counted as hinted, so a learner who is stuck always finishes; the keyboard path stays "Écrire sur papier". The result keeps the learner's strokes, the hinted one in `muted` and named in the caption, and states the grade before Continue, as on the tone card.
- **Writing card, on paper**: the "Écrire sur papier" switch sits in the card's header, the first stop after the card, so a keyboard user reaches it at once. Empty 米 boxes give the number of characters. Space reveals each character with the word page's stroke-order stepper (136 px on a phone, 240 px from 1024 px, play left out under reduced motion), then 1–4 grade with their intervals, "Bien" filled.
- **Typing card**: meaning and audio, no pinyin. The answer field is an ARIA combobox: committed characters sit before the input, the pinyin being typed is in it, underlined with dots. The candidate list below shows the syllables matched ("dian · hua", "guan3 · ton 3"), 9 candidates per page and a button for the next page; the active candidate is outlined in `ink` over `sunken` (candidates carry no tone color). Once checked: the grade, what was typed with its pinyin, then the answer with ruby pinyin and its audio; focus moves to Continue.
- **Typing drill** (Learn): the setup shows the timer switch, off by default; turned on, it reveals the duration (1, 2, 3 or 5 min) as a segmented control. A round reuses the typing card's field without a grade step: the status line reports the previous word, the time left is `role="timer"` and never announced, "Arrêter" ends the round. The result gives the words right and the characters per minute (characters of the right answers over the time spent), then the missed words with their answer, pinyin, meaning and what was typed.
- **Practice settings**: Profile › Apprentissage gains a "Pratique" group with two switches, Saisie on and Écriture à la main off, each with a description, and a line saying that turning one off hides its cards and keeps their history.
- **Switch** (new control): a `button` with `role="switch"` and `aria-checked`, its description tied by `aria-describedby`, the whole 44 px row as target. The 44 × 26 px track is a `border-strong` outline with a `border-strong` thumb at the start when off, filled `ink` with an `on-ink` thumb at the end when on, so the position tells the state as well as the fill.
- **Focus order**: skip link, navigation, then the content in reading order. The card takes focus on arrival, as in review; the drawing grid is not a tab stop, the keyboard path being "Écrire sur papier"; after an automatic grade, focus moves to Continue.

## AI mobilization (M5)

Mocked up for issue #178 from [ADR 0012](../adr/0012-ai-mobilization-byok.md): sixteen states, each on a phone (390 px) and a desktop (1280 px), light and dark, with its focus order. The copy is French, from `apps/web/src/i18n/fr.ts` where a string exists; words, glosses and levels come from the repo's datasets.

- **AI settings** (Profile › "Assistant IA"): the four providers as radio cards, none preselected, each with one line on free use, training on prompts and where the data stays. Groq's name carries a "Recommandé" pill (`border-strong`, 12 px), read with the name (#207). Once one is chosen, a summary row with "Changer" replaces the list when focus leaves it: arrows move through the providers without closing it (WCAG 3.2.2). A key and a model belong to the provider they were set for, so changing provider loses neither and never sends a key elsewhere. The provider notice, a `sunken` aside with the info icon, names the provider, says nothing goes through OpenLinguo, gives its training and storage line and links its privacy policy. The key is a password field with the dedicated-key advice, then the "Ne pas mémoriser la clé" switch; the model picker stays disabled until "Tester la connexion" lists the models; a test with a model chosen then corrects the test sentence 我很好。 with the real correction prompt (no model is picked for the learner: the lists hold speech and embedding models). The model tested by the maintainer reads "openai/gpt-oss-20b (recommandé)" in Groq's list: an option holds text only. Success is a `role="status"` line; each error kind is a `role="alert"` box, 2 px `danger` border and icon, one sentence per kind. A local server has an address field and no key field, with an open `details` giving the `OLLAMA_ORIGINS` line, Chrome's local-network prompt and Safari's block.
- **AI notice**: one native modal dialog, "Avant d'utiliser l'IA", before the first AI call of any kind, "Tester la connexion" included, then never again (#207, was one per feature): a machine writes the answers and can be wrong, to whom and what each AI button sends as a list, and the sensitive-data warning. Focus starts on "J'ai compris, continuer"; Escape or "Annuler" sends nothing.
- **"Envoyé à …" line**: under every AI button, a send icon and what the click sends, tied to the button by `aria-describedby`.
- **AI badge**: "Généré par IA · model", a 12 px pill outlined in `border-strong` with the sparkles icon, beside every AI output: correction, card proposal, suggested words, French glosses.
- **5-sentence challenge**: the last step of the Today session. The word (characters, pinyin, meaning, audio), the input-method choice (the app's pinyin or the device keyboard, remembered), five fields, "Corriger" and "Passer". Without a provider, "Enregistrer" takes the place of "Corriger" and a link leads to the settings. While the call runs the fields are read-only, the button reads "Correction en cours…" (`aria-disabled`, the loader does not spin under reduced motion) and focus moves to "Annuler". An error is the alert box with "Réessayer" and "Plus tard". Saved challenges are listed under Learn › "Défis 5 phrases", the latest day first, each opening the same form with "Enregistrer" (decision 2026-10-19, after the journal's past entries). The punctuation keys of the app's pinyin type full-width marks (。，？！；：) while no pinyin is pending.
- **Correction diff**: an unchanged sentence is one line with a check. Each corrected sentence is a card: the diff, inserted characters underlined 2 px and removed ones struck through in `muted`, each with hidden "ajout" or "suppression" text; the AI's pinyin in tone colors; the meaning; then "Changements", from → to with its explanation in the interface language; then "Accepter" and "Garder la mienne". A legend under the summary says what underline and strike-through mean. A pinyin syllable that is not a CC-CEDICT reading of its character gets a wavy `danger` underline and a sentence naming the dictionary reading, never a silent fix.
- **Card proposal**: sentence, pinyin and meaning, all editable, with the AI badge and the deck picker ("Mes mots" first). A flagged syllable is a field error tied by `aria-describedby`, and "Créer la carte" refuses until it is fixed.
- **Journal** (Learn): today's entry as a text area with the sensitive-data line tied to it, "Corriger" and "Enregistrer", then the past entries as links with a pill, "Corrigée" outlined solid or "Non corrigée" outlined dashed in `muted`.
- **Interests** (Profile › "Centres d'intérêt"): themes as a checkbox grid, two columns on a phone and three from 1024 px, one 120-character field with the sensitive-data help, "Proposer des mots". The results replace the form behind a summary row with "Modifier": each word a checkbox with its characters, pinyin, level pill and dictionary gloss, then the number of discarded suggestions, the deck picker and "Ajouter N mots".
- **Translate to French** (word page): on an entry without a French gloss, "Traduire en français" under the English glosses. The result is a "Français" card with the badge, one French gloss per English sense with its English source under it, and "Supprimer la traduction"; the English card follows. Review cards show the AI gloss with a short "IA" mark.
- **Focus order**: skip link, navigation, then the content in reading order. After a test, focus moves to the model picker; after a correction, to the first "Accepter"; after suggestions, to the first word; after a translation, to the "Français" heading.

## Reader (M6)

Mocked up for issue #223 from [ADR 0014](../adr/0014-graded-reader.md): seventeen states, each on a phone (390 px) and a desktop (1280 px), light and dark, with its focus order; the dictionary's sentence split (#213) is on its own page of the canvas. The copy is French, from `apps/web/src/i18n/fr.ts` where a string exists. Words, readings, levels and glosses come from the repo's datasets (CC-CEDICT, HSK 2025, CFDICT) and the passages are segmented by `lang-zh`'s segmenter; the Wikidata answer for 王芳 was fetched on 2026-10-21. The learner is fictional: HSK 1–3 cards in review and a few « Je connais » marks.

- **Library** (Read tab): « Coller un texte », « Importer un .txt » and « Écrire une histoire » (aria-disabled with a link to Assistant IA when no provider is set), then the texts, latest first. Each text: its title (the learner's, else its first characters) is the link; date and source; the share of known words and the words still to learn; the « Généré par IA » badge for a story; a 44 px delete button named after the text, confirmed by a native modal whose first focus is « Annuler ». « Lire à voix haute » stays below, unchanged. The percentages are computed when the tab opens, from the cards and the marks, so they follow the learner's progress.
- **Paste and import**: one form for both entries, a title (optional, else the first characters), the text in an editable area and a counter against 20,000 characters (provisional, ADR 0014). A `.txt` fills the same form: the file line shows its name, size and encoding (« Lu en GB18030 » when it is not UTF-8, never guessed silently). Over the limit the text still loads so it can be cut (decision 2026-10-21): the error is tied to the field, the counter turns `danger` and « Enregistrer » is aria-disabled. A text in traditional characters gets a non-blocking aside, nothing is converted.
- **Reader**: a coverage block (percentage floored, so 97.6 % never reads 98 %; the 98 % target as a tick on a bar and the gap in points; « N mots à apprendre » and the unresolved sequences counted apart), the pinyin control (Aucun, Mots inconnus by default, Tous: a segmented control), the passage on a `surface` card, then a legend and the keyboard hint. The passage follows ADR 0014: one native button per dictionary word in a `p lang="zh-Hans"`, punctuation as text, one tab stop with ← → Home End. Closing punctuation is kept with the word before it (a `nowrap` group); without that, a button boundary lets a line start with 。.
- **Cues**: an unknown or still-learning word has a 2 px dotted underline in `ink`, read through `aria-describedby` as « mot pas encore connu » (not « nouveau »: a card in its learning steps is not new). The tapped word has a 2 px solid `ink` frame hugging it, with no fill, because tone-coloured pinyin never sits on `sunken`; the focus ring stays the 2 px ring offset by 2 px, so the two never look alike. A tap or Enter opens the word sheet and the word keeps the frame (read as `aria-current`) while it is open; the frame includes the pinyin above the word. The polyphone mark is a small `ink` diamond after the word, 3:1, read as « plusieurs lectures ». **It shows only where a pinyin is shown** (decision 2026-10-21): 17–22 % of the words of a short HSK 1–2 text have several readings in CC-CEDICT (的 了 和 吗 给 说 要…), so a mark on all of them would bury the text; in « Mots inconnus » it marks the unknown polyphones, in « Tous » every one.
- **Pinyin above the characters**: per character, tone-coloured, 14 px, with 2 px of side padding so neighbouring words never touch, over a 56 px line: a 32 px line height plus 24 px above each word, inside the word’s own box, so a reader’s own line height of 1.5 (1.4.12) cannot make the pinyin run into the line above (checked in E2E with ruby on, at 320 px, no fixed height). Chrome, Edge and desktop Safari screen readers do not read `rt`: the word sheet carries the pinyin as text.
- **Word sheet**: the modal `Dialog` as a bottom sheet, 12 px top corners, dimmed backdrop, at most half the viewport with internal scroll, 672 px wide and centred on desktop. Focus starts on the headword heading, polyphone or not (decision 2026-10-24). It holds the characters, the pinyin as text, the HSK pill, the offline glosses, the deck picker (« Mes mots » first), « Ajouter à mes mots », « Je connais ce mot », the word-page link and an empty `role="status"` line filled after an action. A polyphone lists its readings as radio cards with none preselected, each with two lines of glosses; a choice fixes that occurrence and removes the mark, and « Ajouter à mes mots » and « Je connais ce mot » stay `aria-disabled`, with a hint, until one is chosen (decision 2026-10-24). The word stays above the sheet: the page gets half a viewport of room below the passage while the sheet is open and scrolls the word into view. A word with a card in its learning steps says so instead of offering « Ajouter ». After « Je connais ce mot » the button becomes « Je ne connais plus ce mot », keeps focus, and the status line gives the new coverage. Sheet buttons are 44 px.
- **Unknown words** (« Mots inconnus de ce texte · N », a view of its own with « Retour au texte », the same 672 px column on desktop): a sequence is a run of one-character segments with its edges cut on HSK 1–3 words (decision 2026-10-21). On the sample text the raw runs are 她们在袁家界拍了很多 (9 characters) and 王芳和, which is what would reach Wikidata; the cut gives 袁家界拍 and 王芳, and « Corriger » trims what remains. Each card holds the sequence, its sentence with the sequence marked, then proposals, each naming its source (name signal, extended lexicon, Wikidata, AI) with « Accepter », « Modifier » and « Rejeter ». « Chercher sur Wikidata » and « Demander à l'IA » each carry their « Envoyé à … » line, tied by `aria-describedby`. The Wikidata notice is a native modal shown once, before the first call; the AI notice of M5 is not repeated. An answer is kept with its date and shown (« aucun résultat, vérifié le … »). Homonyms: 王芳 returns 32 items on Wikidata, 31 without the disambiguation page; the list shows 5 (French description first, then English, then none), « Voir les 26 autres » opens the rest, and « Aucun de ceux-là » is offered when the card has several Wikidata proposals (decision 2026-10-24: with one, « Rejeter » does the same). An accepted sequence collapses to a line with « Annuler », and a `role="status"` line gives the new coverage. Built for #229 (decisions 2026-10-24): the two local lists download from one button under the intro, with their size (8,6 Mo); runs made only of HSK 1–3 words go last in a closed `details`, « Probablement des mots courants · N », each card flagged « Pas pour Wikidata »; « Corriger » opens the edit form with the sequence to trim; a rejection lasts for the visit.
- **Stories** (« Écrire une histoire »): the learning band (read-only), the theme (the M5 interests), the length (short or medium, a segmented control), the words sent as chips (up to 20, read-only), then « Écrire l'histoire » with its « Envoyé à Groq : … » line. The result opens in the reader with the badge under the title, its coverage, and the names as proposals in the unknown-words panel (« noms à valider »). A valid story is stored at once; an invalid one is the usual error with « Réessayer » and nothing is stored.
- **Words in a text and targets**: words are buttons set in the line and constrained by its height, the WCAG 2.5.8 inline exception recorded under Space, shape, motion; single-character words are 20 px wide and 56 px tall with pinyin above (36 px without). Every other target, sheet buttons included, is 44 px, or the field itself (its label is 24 px).
- **Focus order**: skip link, navigation, then reading order. Reader: back link, « Mots inconnus de ce texte », the pinyin control, the passage (one stop), the legend. Sheet: heading, close, deck, actions, word-page link. Unknown words: back link, the two search buttons, then per sequence « Corriger » and its proposals' buttons.
- **Measured** (Playwright, Chromium, each board rendered at its own width, 2026-10-21): no horizontal overflow on the 68 boards and no target under 24 px outside running text; every one of the 3,296 text elements outside a modal's dimmed backdrop at least 4.74:1 (4.5:1 required); every control border and focus ring at least 3:1, and the dotted underline and the tapped frame too; tone-coloured pinyin at least 4.5:1 on `bg` and `surface` in both themes.

## Accounts and shared library (M7)

Mocked up for issue #267 from [ADR 0015](../adr/0015-backend-and-shared-library.md): twenty-six states, each on a phone (390 px) and a desktop (1280 px), light and dark, with its focus order, on two pages of the canvas. The copy is French and plain enough for teenagers; the sample texts were segmented by `lang-zh` and counted against HSK 2025. Legal page bodies are placeholders for #279.

- **Where it lives** (decisions 2026-10-31): the four tabs stay. Profile gets a « Compte » row opening `/profile/account`; sign-in is `/account/sign-in`, opened from there or from « Soumettre » with a return address. The global library is its own route, `/read/library`, so the Read tab makes no network request; a text opens a preview, `/read/library/:id`. Reading and reporting need no account.
- **Sign-in**: an email field with « Recevoir un code », then « Continuer avec Google » and « Continuer avec GitHub » as equal secondary buttons, a line saying the email leaves from Lettermint without tracking, and the terms and privacy links. The code is one field (`inputmode="numeric"`, `autocomplete="one-time-code"`), not six boxes; the intro gives the address and the 10-minute validity, with no countdown (2.2.1). A wrong code is a field error with the attempts left; an expired or spent code removes the field and shows the alert box with « Recevoir un nouveau code ». « Renvoyer le code » is always offered. The providers' marks are placeholders: #272 uses their official marks.
- **Sign-ups closed**: said only after the code is checked or the provider returns, never right after the address is typed, so the screen does not reveal whether an address has an account. It says no account was created and that the app and the library work without one.
- **Age step**: right after the first sign-in, heading focused, two outlined buttons of equal weight (« J'ai 15 ans ou plus », « J'ai moins de 15 ans »), each with one line saying what it does, and why the question is asked. « Moins de 15 ans » deletes at once without confirmation (the account holds nothing yet) and says the app stays usable without an account.
- **Two-step verification**: set-up in three numbered steps, the QR code on white with a 1 px border in both themes, the key as text in groups of four with « Copier la clé », then the code. Ten backup codes follow, shown once, with « Télécharger (.txt) », « Copier les codes » and « Terminer » aria-disabled until « J'ai rangé ces codes en lieu sûr » is ticked. The prompt is the same single field with « Utiliser un code de secours » and the count of codes left.
- **Compte**: sections for the sign-in method with « Se déconnecter », the pseudonym, two-step verification, « Mes textes envoyés », « Mes données » (JSON export) and deletion; an admin also gets « Modération ». The pseudonym is empty until the learner signs a text and is never taken from Google or GitHub (decision 2026-10-31). A sent text shows its status and its actions: pending, « Retirer la soumission »; published, « Retirer de la bibliothèque » and « Ne plus afficher mon pseudonyme »; rejected or removed, « Voir la décision ». A sent text cannot be edited: it is withdrawn and sent again (decision 2026-10-31).
- **Account deletion**: a modal, first focus on « Annuler », a radio group for published texts with « Le garder, sans nom » checked by default, a line saying imported copies stay (CC BY-SA cannot be withdrawn), and, for an account that submitted, an aside naming what décret 2021-1362 makes us keep and for how long.
- **Submit** (`/read/$id/submit`, a page, not a dialog, because it is longer than a phone screen): the text, the licence fixed by the origin as a pill with three plain lines and a link (CC BY-SA 4.0 for a text the learner wrote, CC0 1.0 for a story), the signature (« Anonyme » by default; choosing the pseudonym with none set opens the field in place), one checkbox per attested statement (attestation v1), a reminder to leave out anything that identifies someone, and a preview of the library card. A refusal from the server (five texts pending, offline) is the alert box at the top, focused, with the answers kept.
- **Statuses**: on the Read list a sent text carries a pill: « Publié » solid with a check, « En attente de relecture » dashed in `muted` with a clock, « Refusé » solid with a cross. A rejection is a state, so it carries no `danger` colour. « Bibliothèque globale » is a link row under the three Read actions.
- **Global library**: a level filter (native select), then cards with the title link, a pill « HSK 3 · estimé », the AI badge for a story, origin and attribution in words, date and licence link, « Importer » and « Signaler », each named after its text. The preview shows the passage as plain text, the learner's coverage computed on the device for that text only, and the licence block with what reuse requires. After import the button becomes « Déjà dans Mes textes » (aria-disabled) and a status line offers « Ouvrir dans le lecteur ». Offline or a server error is the alert box with « Réessayer »; nothing stale is shown as fresh.
- **Report form** (`/report`, no account, DSA art. 16(2)): the reported text and its address read-only, a reason (the moderation checklist's illegal categories plus « Ne respecte pas les règles de la bibliothèque »), an explanation, name and email (optional only for child sexual abuse material), the good-faith checkbox, and the reminder of LCEN art. 6 VII (1 year and €15,000). The receipt gives the notice number and what happens next, and is also emailed when an address was given.
- **Moderation** (`/admin`, 880 px column; 1040 px for a review, text and panel side by side): a queue with two tabbed links, « Textes en attente » and « Signalements », oldest first. A review shows the estimated level and a table of the text's words by HSK level, computed in the admin's browser; the rule is #274's. Then come the metadata and a six-point checklist (copyright, defamation, hate, other people's personal data, LCEN art. 6 IV.A offences, library rules); « Approuver » stays aria-disabled until all six are ticked. A rejection or removal fills the statement of reasons of DSA art. 17(3): the measure as fixed text, its origin (a notice or the admin's own initiative), the ground in two optgroups (« Contenu illégal » with its legal reference, « Règles de la bibliothèque » with its clause number), the facts, a plain explanation, automated means fixed to « aucun », and fixed redress (a review by email, the courts).
- **Legal pages**: one template for `/legal`, `/privacy` and `/terms`: back link, title, update date, a link to the English version, then h2 sections. `/privacy` and `/terms` have a table of contents (an ordered list of links in a `nav`), above the text on a phone and in a 240 px column beside it from 1024 px (960 px in all). `/terms` numbers its rules (3.1 illegal content, 3.2 not in Chinese, 3.3 duplicate or spam, 3.4 unsuitable for teenagers) because a statement of reasons cites them; `/privacy` holds the retention table of ADR 0015.
- **Focus order**: skip link, navigation, then reading order. On arrival, focus goes to the first field of a form, to the alert after an error, and to the heading of a result page (sign-ups closed, age step, account deleted, receipt). In a modal, focus starts on « Annuler ».
- **Measured** (Playwright, Chromium, each board at its own width, 2026-10-31): no horizontal overflow on the 104 boards and no target under 24 px; checkboxes and radios are 24 px with their label as part of the target. Every one of the 2,354 text elements outside a modal's dimmed backdrop is at least 6.12:1. Links under 44 px sit only inside a sentence or the date-and-licence line of a card.

## Accessibility rules

- `lang="zh-Hans"` on Chinese text and `lang="zh-Latn-pinyin"` on pinyin.
- Stroke drawing always has a single-pointer, keyboard-operable alternative (WCAG 2.5.1, 2.5.7).
- Every microphone exercise offers a non-speech alternative without penalty.
- No mandatory time limit (WCAG 2.2.1).
- Curves, trees and heatmaps have a text equivalent.
