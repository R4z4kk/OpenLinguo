# DPIA screening

Screening against the nine criteria of the Article 29 Working Party guidelines on DPIA (WP248 rev.01, endorsed by the EDPB). As a rule of thumb, processing meeting two or more criteria is likely to require a DPIA.

| # | Criterion | Applies? | Reasoning |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Evaluation or scoring | **Possibly** | Leagues rank learners by XP; progress statistics evaluate learning. Opt-in, no consequence outside the app |
| 2 | Automated decision with legal or similar significant effect | No | Scheduling and rankings have no legal or similar effect |
| 3 | Systematic monitoring | No | No tracking; review logs are synced only on opt-in |
| 4 | Sensitive or highly personal data | No on the server | Voice is processed on the device only and never uploaded |
| 5 | Large scale | No, for now | Small open-source project; reassess if accounts grow significantly |
| 6 | Matching or combining datasets | No | No data enrichment or cross-referencing |
| 7 | Vulnerable data subjects | **Possibly** | Accounts open to 15–17-year-olds |
| 8 | Innovative use of technology | No on the server | AI runs through the user's own provider; speech runs on the device |
| 9 | Prevents exercising a right or using a service | No | The app works fully without an account |

## Conclusion

Two criteria may apply (1 and 7). A DPIA is therefore carried out before accounts open in M7, focused on leagues and teenage users. Mitigations already designed in: opt-in leagues, pseudonyms and predefined avatars only, no messaging, no profiling, no ads, accounts only from 15, minimal server data.
