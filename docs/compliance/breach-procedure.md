# Personal data breach procedure

Applies to any breach of security leading to the accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, unauthorised disclosure of, or access to, personal data held by the server (accounts, submissions, synced data, leagues, logs, backups).

## Steps

1. **Contain.** Revoke exposed secrets and sessions, block the attack path, take the API offline if needed. Local-first means learners keep working offline.
2. **Assess.** What data, how many people, what likely consequences, is there a risk to their rights and freedoms? Is the risk high?
3. **Record.** Add an entry to the internal register below, notified or not (GDPR art. 33(5)).
4. **Notify the CNIL within 72 hours** of becoming aware, unless the breach is unlikely to result in a risk (art. 33(1)). Use the online service at [notifications.cnil.fr](https://notifications.cnil.fr/). If facts are incomplete, send an initial notification and complete it later, explaining any delay. The notification describes (art. 33(3)):
   - the nature of the breach, categories and approximate number of people and records concerned;
   - the contact point for more information;
   - the likely consequences;
   - the measures taken or proposed, including mitigation.
5. **Inform affected people without undue delay if the risk is high** (art. 34(1)), in plain language, by email and in the app. When unsure, notify the CNIL, which indicates whether people must be informed.
6. **Review.** Root cause, fix, and update the [data map](data-map.md), [records](records-of-processing.md) or security measures as needed.
