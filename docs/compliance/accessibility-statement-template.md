## Built-in alternatives

- Stroke drawing: stroke-order quiz without dragging.
- Microphone exercises: "I can't speak right now" option without penalty.
- Timed challenges: timers can be turned off.
- Tones: always written with diacritics; colors can be turned off.
- Charts and trees: text equivalents.
